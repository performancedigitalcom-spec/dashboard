      How these numbers are counted. Impressions and clicks come straight from each ad platform. Clicks on Meta are link clicks. Conversions add Google Ads conversions to the leads, calls and messaging conversations Meta attributes to a campaign; TikTok campaigns here are traffic campaigns with no conversion goal. Programmatic (Illumin) conversions include view-through conversions and are shown in their own section, not in the conversion total. Click-through rate across all platforms includes CTV and audio, which are rarely clicked. Website analytics come from GA4 for the clients whose property is linked in the PD warehouse. Audience and video numbers come from monthly platform reports, so a time frame counts every calendar month it touches. Pacing compares this month’s spend with last month’s spend per active day; the projection adds the last 7 days’ daily average for each day left. Highlights compare each client with the period before and with the PD average for the same platform. “vs prior” compares to the same number of days just before the time frame (for a calendar month, the month before). Investment is what each platform reports as spent.
